        How the names work

        Elves of the Realms

        In the world of Faerûn, surface elves have several lineages, the most common being high elves (sun and moon elves) and wood elves. While no distinct naming conventions are explicitly documented for these lineages, patterns can be observed in published material, and this generator reflects those patterns.

        Elves are given childhood names at birth and choose their adult names some time after their hundredth birthday (Player's Handbook, 5e). This generator creates only adult names, but childhood names are generally shorter. Although an elf's childhood name does not have to carry over into adulthood, the prefixes generated here could often serve as childhood names.

        First names are built from the elven name tables in Races of the Wild (3.5e), first published as "By Any Other Name" (Dragon #251): a prefix and one or more suffixes, each with its own meaning. When two components don't join smoothly, a letter is added, as that article suggests. Together with the family names, that allows over a quadrillion theoretically possible combinations. With Expanded Variety on, some prefixes and suffixes are instead the start or end of a documented Elven name; their meanings are unknown.

        Every elf has a family name, often translated into Common (Player's Handbook, 5e). This generator uses published family names (with their translation when a book gives one, such as Galanodel, "Moonwhisper"), builds new ones from the same tables, or combines Common words found in published family names. In published sources, moon elves use Common family names more frequently than sun elves, and the generator reflects that distinction.

        No two elves share a name except by choice (The Complete Book of Elves, 2e). If a generated name matches that of a published character, it is flagged so you know.

        A small number of names come from Ed Greenwood's answers to fans on the Candlekeep forums rather than from official books. He created the Forgotten Realms, but his answers are not official publications, so names that come only from them are labelled.

        Drow

        Drow are dark elves whose ancestral homeland is the Underdark. While they share a common ancestry with surface elves, they have developed their own distinct culture and traditions.

        Drow names are constructed using the tables in "By Any Other Name: The Drow" (Dragon #267), combining a prefix with one or more suffixes, each carrying its own meaning. Traditional drow naming conventions distinguish between masculine and feminine name elements, reflecting the rigid gender hierarchy of Lolthite society. Feminine elements carry associations of status and authority: a drow man who used one would be considered "a rogue or troublemaker."

        While noble families proudly bear their house surnames, commoners generally cannot use a noble house name unless they hold a titled position within that house (The Drow of the Underdark, 2e). This generator uses published house names or constructs new ones from the tables in Dragon #267. By tradition, no house name begins with the same "L" sound as Lolth, and no drow bears her name or its variants, except a revealed avatar of the goddess (The Drow of the Underdark, 2e).

        Espruar

        Espruar is the elven alphabet of Faerûn, the script elves use to write their languages (Forgotten Realms Campaign Setting, 3e). It is an alphabet, not a language.

        Under each name, the same letters are shown in an Espruar font. This is a letter-for-letter transcription, not a translation. Drow names are shown in the High Drowic font instead.
